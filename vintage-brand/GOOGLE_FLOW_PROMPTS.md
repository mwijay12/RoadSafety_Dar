# Google Flow / Veo Prompt Pack — VINTAGE JUICE POINT

Use these prompts to make short premium video assets in **Google Flow**. I cannot render the video from here, so this pack is written to paste directly into Flow.

## How to use

1. Create a new Flow project.
2. Upload the images in `flow-inputs/` as style / reference frames.
3. Choose a cinematic video model (Veo if offered).
4. Generate **8-second clips** one scene at a time.
5. Use **9:16** for Reels / TikTok / Shorts and **16:9** for YouTube / website hero.
6. Paste the **style anchor** before each scene prompt.
7. Stitch the clips in the Flow editor or your editor of choice.
8. **Do not ask the video model to render readable text or logos.** Add the real logo, tagline and contact details as overlays in post-production using the supplied SVG/PNG assets.

## Master 30-second brand film prompt

Copy this as one prompt if you want a single longer generation, or break it into the six scene prompts below.

```text
Create a 30-second premium brand film for VINTAGE JUICE POINT, a fresh-juice bar in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. Deep charcoal plaster, warm brushed brass, terracotta walls, Swahili-coast geometric shadows, golden-hour coastal light. Use the uploaded reference images for juice colour, bottle silhouette, fruit and black/gold/terracotta mood, but do not copy the old logo, script fonts or busy menu.

0–4s: extreme macro of deep red roselle juice pouring slowly into a clear glass bottle; condensation beads form; warm light rims the glass; camera drifts 2 cm to the right.
4–9s: elegant still-life cut of halved passion fruit, mango cubes, watermelon triangle, grapes, roselle and avocado on dark stone; a hand places one orange leaf; slow push-in.
9–14s: top-down signature blend being built in layers — mango gold, pineapple, passion fruit, avocado green — in a clear cup; ingredients settle in slow motion; soft brass tray edge visible.
14–19s: a hand with a delicate gold bangle lifts a tall clear cup of roselle juice against a terracotta wall; a geometric kanga-like shadow moves gently across the wall; hold on negative space at the top.
19–24s: wide interior shot of a calm juice counter at golden hour: dark plaster, brass counter edge, carved Zanzibar-door-inspired panel, three unbranded juice bottles and a small bowl of fruit; slow dolly forward; no people, no signage text.
24–30s: return to the hero still life of three juices — roselle red, mango gold, avocado green — on dark stone with fruit arranged sparingly; camera slowly rises; end on a clean, static frame with generous empty space on the left for a logo overlay.

Cinematic, tactile, quiet luxury, 24fps feel, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, no rapid cuts. Do not render any readable text, logo, phone number, email or watermark. End frame must be suitable for adding the VINTAGE JUICE POINT logo and the line “Asili safi. Ladha ya kisasa.” in post.
```

## Scene 1 — The Pour · 8s · 9:16

```text
Extreme macro, vertical frame. Deep crimson roselle juice pours in a slow ribbon into a chilled clear glass bottle on dark charcoal stone. Tiny condensation beads, warm Dar es Salaam late-afternoon light catching the rim, a faint brass tray edge out of focus behind. Camera drifts almost imperceptibly to the right. One continuous take, no cuts, no hands in frame, no text, no logo. Tactile, quiet, premium.
```

## Scene 2 — From the Coast · 8s · 16:9

```text
Slow push-in on a refined still life: halved passion fruit, diced mango, watermelon triangle, dark grapes, roselle calyx, orange slice and avocado half arranged sparingly on dark stone. A single hand enters from the right and places a fresh orange leaf, then exits. Golden-hour light, soft shadow, subtle brass line in background. No clutter, no text, no logo, no illustrated fruit.
```

## Scene 3 — Signature Build · 8s · 9:16

```text
Top-down vertical shot of a clear smoothie cup on a dark surface. In layers, mango-passion gold, pineapple, passionfruit seeds, avocado-pineapple pale green and ice settle into the cup in gentle slow motion. A thin brass line and a small roselle flower sit beside the cup. Clean negative space at the top. Camera slowly rotates 5 degrees. No spoon, no text, no logo, no plastic straw.
```

## Scene 4 — The Handoff · 8s · 9:16

```text
A hand with a delicate gold bangle holds a tall clear cup of deep roselle juice against a textured terracotta wall. Sunlight casts a soft geometric Swahili-coast shadow across the wall; the shadow moves slightly as if a breeze moved leaves outside. The cup has condensation and no label. Hold generous negative space above the cup for a future logo overlay. Warm, human, premium, no readable text, no watermark.
```

## Scene 5 — Night Counter · 8s · 16:9

```text
Wide cinematic interior of a small premium juice counter in Dar es Salaam at golden hour: dark charcoal plaster wall, brushed brass counter edge, subtle carved geometric panel inspired by Zanzibar doors, terracotta floor tile, three unbranded juice bottles in roselle red, mango gold and avocado green, one small wooden bowl of fruit. Blank dark signage rectangle above the counter with no lettering. Slow dolly forward, warm window light, no people, no text, no logo.
```

## Scene 6 — End Frame · 8s · 9:16

```text
Static vertical hero frame: three clear bottles and glasses of roselle red, mango gold and avocado green juice on dark charcoal stone, fresh passion fruit, mango, grapes and avocado arranged sparingly, warm brass tray edge at the right, generous clean negative space on the left and top. Light slowly warms by 10 percent over 8 seconds. Almost no camera movement. No text, no logo, no watermark. Designed for a post-production logo and tagline overlay.
```
